Pre-release notice: AIVS Agent Bridge is a proposed independent connector. The service and associated integrations are not yet generally available. This policy describes the planned operation and must be reviewed and updated to match the production implementation before personal information is collected.

This Privacy Policy describes how AIVS Agent Bridge (“we”, “our” or the “Service”) intends to collect, use and share information when users access its connector, related website, APIs and support channels.

1. Information involved

Depending on enabled features and user choices, the Service may process contact and account information, authentication identifiers, user-submitted prompts, project notes, code snippets, task outputs, error reports and technical information such as request timestamps and IP addresses. Access to third-party content will require the relevant authorization. We do not intend to request or receive users’ existing ChatGPT web session cookies or private ChatGPT conversation history.

2. How information is used

Information will be used to authenticate requests, deliver requested AI or connector operations, maintain task state if enabled, prevent misuse, diagnose failures, respond to support requests and comply with applicable law. Before general availability, the actual operational data flows and retention periods will be documented.

3. AI providers and other third parties

When a user authorizes a supported integration, task inputs and necessary metadata may be transmitted to service providers, which may include Muse, OpenAI API, hosting providers and the project’s authorized task-execution services. Such providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. This proposed connector is independent of Meta and OpenAI and does not provide direct access to a user’s ChatGPT account, Plus subscription, or conversation history.

4. Information sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with vendors necessary to operate the Service; with user-authorized connected services; where required by applicable law; or when reasonably necessary to protect security and prevent fraud. We will update this policy before materially changing these practices.

5. Data retention

When the connector launches, retention will be limited to what is reasonably needed for its operation, security, troubleshooting and legal compliance. The exact retention schedule and user deletion process must be finalized and disclosed before public operation. Third-party services may maintain separate retention policies.

6. Security

We plan to use appropriate access controls and secure communications for deployed services. No transmission or storage system is completely secure. Detailed production safeguards will be documented after implementation and testing; we do not claim the proposed connector has already passed a security audit.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

Applicable law may provide rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information. For general inquiries or to request a private contact method, use our support issue tracker. Do not post passwords, private data or confidential privacy requests in public GitHub issues. A confidential privacy contact will be provided before the production service begins processing user data.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for children who cannot independently consent to its use under applicable law. We will not knowingly process children’s personal data in violation of applicable law.

9. International transfers

Service providers may process authorized task data in countries other than the user’s location. Deployment locations and applicable safeguards will be identified before public operation.

10. Updates

We may update this policy as the Service develops. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the date when changes take effect.

11. Contact

General support: FirstWindow / GitHub Issues. For confidential requests, ask for a private contact channel without including sensitive details in your issue.
